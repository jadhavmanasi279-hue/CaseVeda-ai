
# CaseVeda AI ⚖️

### AI-Powered Court Judgment Analysis and Evidence Verification

## 📌 Problem Statement

Indian court judgments can be lengthy and difficult to understand. Finding important case details, applicable legal sections, and final decisions can take considerable time.

## 💡 Our Solution

CaseVeda AI aims to analyze court judgment PDFs and extract important information while linking each extracted fact to supporting evidence from the original document.

## ✨ Key Features

* Upload court judgment PDFs.
* Extract important case details.
* Identify parties, legal sections, and final outcomes.
* Link extracted facts to supporting passages and page numbers.
* Flag missing or uncertain information for manual verification.
* Generate an easy-to-understand case summary.

## 🛠️ Planned Technologies

* Frontend: To be decided
* Backend: Python
* PDF Processing: PyMuPDF
* AI Integration: An existing AI API

## 🎯 Project Goal

To make court judgments easier to understand and legal information more transparent and verifiable.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

CaseVeda AI is intended as a research and document-analysis aid, not a substitute for professional legal advice.
